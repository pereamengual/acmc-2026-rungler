THE RUNGLER

Feedback as instrument.

Exploring the Rungler, nonlinear modulation, and the creative possibilities of recursive electronic systems.

A 90-minute hands-on workshop inspired by Rob Hordijk’s instrument designs. Participants will investigate the transition from stochastic variation to clocked structures, nonlinear feedback, and cross-coupled synthesis.

01 / APPROACH

Beyond fixed grids.

What happens when a system’s behavior arises from its internal feedback rather than from an externally imposed sequence?

This workshop puts stochastic processes, sample-and-hold, deterministic feedback, and quantization side by side. The goal is to listen, observe, and ask what each architecture enables—and what it constrains.

02 / LEARNING PATH

Six stages of exploration.

The following modules describe the planned hands-on exercises. Interactive controls and sound generation are not yet enabled in this preview.

  1. 00A

    Continuous noise

    Unclocked stochastic variation as a point of comparison: fluctuating signals without an explicit stored state.

    STOCHASTIC
  2. 00B

    Clocked sample & hold

    Sampling, discrete steps, and an optional diatonic quantizer: where regularity enters the system.

    CLOCKED
  3. 01

    The core Rungler

    Coupled oscillators, a shift register, feedback, and phase-space exploration of changing system behavior.

    RECURSIVE
  4. 02

    PWM acoustic generation

    Comparator interference and pulse-width modulation as audible consequences of interacting control signals.

    COMPARATOR
  5. 03

    Exciter & resonator

    Routing complex excitation into the Twin Peak Filter to explore resonances and vocal-like spectral structures.

    RESONANCE
  6. 04

    Blippoo Box architecture

    Two cross-coupled Rungler systems and recursive modulation across a larger instrument topology.

    SYSTEM
